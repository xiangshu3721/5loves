# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React. Confirmed by the user.

## Users

Individuals who want to learn how they prefer to receive love, then optionally send the result to a partner. The first session is solo. Sharing is a second beat, not a two-player interface.

## Product Purpose

A mobile-first online version of the printed 30-pair forced-choice “爱的5种语言” assessment. Success is finishing all 30 items without friction and leaving with a primary language, five scores, and readable interpretation.

## Positioning

The mechanism is Chapman-style forced choice: each of 30 items presents two statements; the person must pick the one that feels more true. Scores tally letters A–E into the five languages. Neighboring quizzes that use Likert scales or self-report labels are not this product.

## Operating Context

Used on a phone, often in a quiet personal moment or while chatting with a partner. No account. Progress should survive a refresh during the 30 questions. Result links should reopen the same scores without retaking the test.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 30 forced-choice pairs from the source infographic, scored A 肯定的言词 / B 精心的时刻 / C 接受礼物 / D 服务的行动 / E 身体的接触.
- Progress through items one pair at a time on mobile, with a visible progress indicator.
- Results: five scores, primary language (highest score; ties disclosed), bar or radar distribution, and the source interpretations for how to express that language.
- Result can be opened via a generated link.
- No login, no backend, no payment.
- Open: whether to store answers locally beyond the current session besides the share link.

## Brand Commitments

Chinese product name: 爱的5种语言. Subtitle from the source: 你们表达爱的方式，真的对了吗？ Visual direction is not decided here; the infographic is source content, not a locked brand system.

## Evidence on Hand

Source infographic at the conversation asset (30 pairs, scoring letters, interpretation table). Do not invent Chapman book quotes, testimonials, or claims of official certification.

## Product Principles

- Forced choice stays forced: never allow skipping, “both”, or Likert.
- Mobile thumb reach first: large tap targets, one decision on screen.
- Scores are the product: the result must show all five languages, not only the winner.
- Share is a snapshot of scores, not a second test-taker flow.
- Interpretation teaches how to give love in that language, not a personality fortune.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on controls and body text. Honor prefers-reduced-motion. Do not assume heterosexual couples in copy; “对方 / 心爱的人” stays as in the source items.
